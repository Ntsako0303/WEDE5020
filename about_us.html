Introduction

Zara is a global fashion brand known for its trendy, affordable clothing.
It is inspired by the latest fashion trends, that offer clothing for childern, men, and women.

History

Amancio Oretga founded Zara in 1975 in Spain. It started as a small store and grew into one of the world's biggest fashion retailers.

Mission/Vision

Zara aims to provide stylish,high-quality fashion at affordable prices while quickly responding to changing trends.

What makes Zara Unique

  • Fast fashion model
  • Regularly updates collections
  • Combines quality with affordablitiy
  • Global presence in many countries
